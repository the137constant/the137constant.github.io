The name

Why 137?

“137” references α⁻¹ ≈ 137.036, the inverse of the dimensionless fine-structure constant α, which characterizes the strength of electromagnetic coupling. Its value has fascinated physicists for generations because, despite being measured with extraordinary precision, there is still no accepted explanation for why nature gives it this value.

The reference is naming inspiration only. This token makes no scientific claim and performs no scientific function. It is a standard ERC-20 token and is not related to ERC-137, the Ethereum Name Service specification.

Liquidity and allocation

  • The creator allocation and ecosystem reserve are an accounting designation only. Both sit at the same address under the same key. They are not vested, escrowed, time-locked, or cryptographically separated.
  • The LP position is retained by the treasury. It is not locked and not burned. The Treasury controls the LP position and can remove liquidity, receiving the underlying WETH and 137.
  • The pool is shallow. It launched with roughly $600 of total liquidity, so small trades move the price substantially. Any displayed price or market capitalisation should be read with that in mind.
  • The token has not been audited by an external security firm.
  • No return, yield, price appreciation, or future value is promised or implied.
