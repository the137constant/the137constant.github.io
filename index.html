About the name

“137” references the approximate value of the inverse fine-structure constant (about 137.036), a dimensionless constant in physics associated with the strength of the electromagnetic interaction. The reference is naming inspiration only. This token makes no scientific claim and performs no scientific function. It is a standard ERC-20 token and is not related to ERC-137, an unrelated Ethereum specification.
